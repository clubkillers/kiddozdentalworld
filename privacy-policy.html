Privacy Notice

This notice explains how Kiddoz Dental World handles the information you share through our online appointment form.

What we collect

When you book an appointment with us, we ask for:

  • Child's name and age
  • Parent's or guardian's name
  • Phone / WhatsApp number
  • Reason for visit
  • Preferred appointment date

Why we collect it

We use these details only to contact you about your requested appointment — to confirm timing, follow up on your enquiry, and prepare for your child's visit. We do not use this information for advertising, retargeting, or any third-party marketing, and no ad-tracking or retargeting pixels are attached to our appointment form.

How long we keep it

We retain appointment and enquiry details only for as long as needed to arrange and provide your child's dental care, and to meet our clinical record-keeping obligations. Information that is no longer needed for these purposes is securely deleted.

Who sees it

Your information is only accessible to Dr. Nipun Jain and authorised Kiddoz Dental World staff directly involved in scheduling and providing your child's care. We do not sell or share your details with third parties.

Your consent and how to withdraw it

By ticking the consent box on our appointment form, you confirm that you are the child's parent or legal guardian and agree to let us use the details you provide to contact you about that appointment. You can withdraw your consent, or ask us to access, correct, or delete your information, at any time by contacting us:

  • Email: Drnipun@kiddozdentalworld.com
