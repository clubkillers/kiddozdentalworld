Orthodontics (Braces & Aligners)

Dental Braces

Dental braces are used to correct crooked, crowded, or misaligned teeth and to fix bite problems such as overbite, underbite, and crossbite. Early orthodontic treatment in growing children helps guide proper jaw development and prevents more serious dental issues in the future.Dental Braces help to straighten crooked or overlapping teeth, correct spacing issues, enhance facial balance and smile aesthetics.

Dr. Nipun Jain carefully examines your child’s teeth and jaw development to recommend the right time and type of orthodontic treatment, ensuring the best long-term results.

Invisible Braces & Clear Aligners

For children and teenagers who prefer a more discreet and modern solution for straightening teeth, Invisible Braces (Clear Aligners) are an excellent option.
These are specially designed, transparent trays that are custom-made to gradually move the teeth into the correct position without the look and feel of traditional metal braces.

Benefits of Invisible Braces:

  • Almost invisible appearance

  • Removable for eating & brushing

  • More comfortable than
    metal brackets

  • Easy to maintain oral hygiene

  • No food restrictions like with regular braces

Space Maintainers

When a child loses a baby tooth earlier than expected, the adjacent teeth can shift into the empty space, causing alignment problems in the future.

A space maintainer is a simple device that plays a major role in preventing the need for major orthodontic treatment later.

Early Orthodontic Evaluation

Orthodontic problems are best corrected when identified early. We provide early orthodontic assessment for:

  • Crooked or crowded teeth

  • Improper jaw growth

  • Bite problems (overbite, underbite, crossbite)
