Invisalign Aligners

What is Invisalign?

Invisalign is a modern clear aligner system that gently guides teeth into the correct position using a series of smooth, custom-made transparent trays — no metal brackets or wires involved. Each set of aligners is designed specifically for your child's teeth and is changed every few weeks as the smile gradually straightens.

For children and teenagers who feel self-conscious about traditional metal braces, Invisalign offers a nearly invisible way to fix crowding, spacing and mild bite issues while they continue school, sports and everyday activities with confidence.

How Invisalign Works for Kids & Teens

Dr. Nipun Jain begins with a detailed evaluation of your child's teeth, jaw growth and bite to check whether Invisalign is the right fit.
Digital scans are used to plan the exact tooth movements in advance, and a custom series of aligners is created — worn for about 20–22 hours a day and removed only for eating, brushing and flossing.

Benefits of Invisalign:

  • Nearly invisible appearance

  • Removable for eating & brushing

  • Smooth plastic — no wire pokes or metal irritation

  • Easier to maintain oral hygiene

  • No food restrictions like with metal braces

Is Invisalign Right for Your Child?

Every child's smile is different. During a consultation, Dr. Nipun Jain checks whether Invisalign is a suitable option, or whether another course of treatment would work better, based on:

  • Mild to moderate crowding or spacing

  • Stage of jaw and tooth development

  • Your child's ability to wear aligners consistently each day
