Meet the Doctor

Meet Your Child’s Smile Superhero
Dr. Nipun Jain

Pediatric & Preventive Dentist
(Amrita School of Dentistry, Kochi)

At Kiddoz Dental World, we know that going to the dentist can feel a little scary for children. That’s why we’ve created a space where kids feel safe, relaxed, and even excited about their dental visit!

Leading this happy, child-friendly clinic is Dr. Nipun Jain, a specially trained Pediatric & Preventive Dentist who loves working with children and understanding their unique needs.

With his calm nature, friendly approach, and playful communication, your child is always in kind and capable hands.

Why Parents Trust Dr. Nipun Jain

  • Specially trained to treat babies, toddlers, children & teenagers
  • Loves explaining dental care in a fun, kid-friendly way
  • Expert in painless and gentle dental care
  • Focuses on prevention, healthy habits & strong smiles
  • Uses “Happy Air” (laughing gas) to reduce fear & anxiety
  • Educates parents about the best oral care practices
