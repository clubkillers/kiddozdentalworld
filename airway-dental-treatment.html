Airway Dental Treatment

What is Airway-Focused Dentistry?

A child's ability to breathe freely through the nose plays a big role in how the jaw, teeth and face develop — and in how well they sleep. When breathing is restricted, children often compensate by breathing through the mouth, which over time can affect jaw growth, tooth alignment and sleep quality.

Airway-focused dentistry looks beyond cavities and crooked teeth to screen for these early signs, so that breathing and growth-related concerns can be identified and addressed as early as possible.

Signs Your Child May Need an Airway Evaluation

Many airway-related habits go unnoticed because they seem harmless. Parents should watch out for:

Mouth breathing during the day or while asleep, snoring or restless sleep, dark circles under the eyes, frequent tiredness or trouble concentrating at school, a tongue-tie, and a narrow or high-arched palate.

Why Early Airway Screening Matters:

  • Better, more restful sleep

  • Improved jaw & facial development

  • Easier, nose-first breathing

  • Reduced risk of future orthodontic issues

  • Better focus & behaviour at school

Our Airway Evaluation Includes

Airway concerns are best managed with an early, team-based approach. Dr. Nipun Jain's evaluation covers:

  • Clinical assessment of breathing & sleep patterns

  • Tongue-tie / lip-tie & palate screening

  • Referral coordination with ENT specialists & myofunctional therapists when needed
