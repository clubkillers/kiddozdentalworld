Behaviour & Anxiety Management

Happy Air – Conscious Sedation (Laughing Gas)

Nitrous oxide (Happy Air) is a mild sedative gas breathed through a small nose mask. It can help some anxious children stay relaxed during dental treatment. The child stays awake and responds to instructions. The effect wears off within minutes of stopping the gas.

Possible side effects include nausea, dizziness and sweating. It does not suit every child, for example a child with a blocked nose or certain medical conditions, so the dentist assesses suitability and explains the procedure to parents before treatment. The child is monitored throughout.

Special Care/Anxious Child Management

Some children may feel scared or anxious about dental treatment. At Kiddoz Dental World, we use:

  • Behaviour management techniques

  • Positive reinforcement

  • Calm, comforting environment

  • Child psychology- based communication

These approaches aim to help the child cooperate with treatment.
