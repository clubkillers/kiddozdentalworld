SPECIALISED PROCEDURES

Pediatric Dental Trauma Management

Dental injuries are common in children, especially during play, sports and everyday accidents. A chipped, fractured, loosened, displaced or knocked-out tooth needs timely assessment to protect the developing teeth and surrounding tissues. At Kiddoz Dental World, we provide specialised evaluation and treatment for dental trauma in children, with the goal of relieving discomfort, preserving the affected tooth whenever possible and supporting healthy development of the permanent teeth.

Pulp Therapy for Milk Teeth

Deep cavities in baby teeth can sometimes reach the dental pulp, causing pain, sensitivity or infection. Pediatric pulp therapy, including pulpotomy and pulpectomy, helps treat the affected portion of the tooth while preserving the tooth whenever possible. Maintaining healthy baby teeth is important for chewing, speech, space maintenance and the normal development of permanent teeth.

Happy Air (Nitrous Oxide) Sedation

Some children may feel nervous, fearful or uncomfortable during dental treatment. Nitrous oxide sedation, commonly known as Happy Air, is a gentle technique that can help children feel more relaxed during dental procedures. The child remains conscious and responsive throughout treatment, while the dental team monitors them carefully. It can be particularly helpful for children with dental anxiety or those requiring longer or more involved procedures.

Minimally Invasive Pediatric Dentistry

Modern pediatric dentistry focuses not only on treating cavities but also on preserving healthy tooth structure wherever possible. Minimally invasive techniques aim to manage dental decay with conservative treatment while reducing unnecessary removal of healthy tooth structure. Depending on the condition of the tooth, treatment may include preventive approaches, fluoride therapy, sealants, SDF or conservative restorations.

Pediatric Zirconia & Bioflex Crowns

When a child's tooth has extensive decay, significant damage or has undergone pulp therapy, a pediatric crown can help restore its strength and function. Zirconia and Bioflex crowns are modern pediatric crown options that provide durable coverage while offering a natural-looking appearance. The choice of crown depends on the child's age, tooth condition, bite, clinical requirements and overall treatment plan.

Dental Treatment Under General Anesthesia

For selected children, routine dental treatment may not be possible because of very young age, severe dental anxiety, extensive treatment needs or certain special-care requirements. In such cases, dental treatment under general anesthesia (GA) may be considered after a thorough evaluation. It allows comprehensive treatment to be completed in a controlled hospital environment with appropriate medical and anesthesia support, when clinically indicated.
